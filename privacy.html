Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Tazzuba Learn is run by Tazzuba. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

When you sign in with Pi, we receive your Pi username and a Pi user ID. If you subscribe, Pi Network processes the payment and we receive the payment ID and status. We do not receive your wallet passphrase or private keys.

Your lesson progress and streak are stored on your device. Questions you send to the AI tutor are sent to our server and to our AI provider to produce an answer; we do not use them to identify you.

How we use it

To sign you in, unlock lessons you have paid for, answer tutor questions, and improve the lessons. We do not sell your data or show third-party ads.

How long we keep it

We keep account and payment records while your account is active and as long as needed for accounting. You can ask us to delete your data at any time by emailing us.

Children

Tazzuba Learn is meant for adults who use Pi Network.
